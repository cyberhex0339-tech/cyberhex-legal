How to request deletion

If you want to request deletion of information associated with the Cyber HeX Meta application, follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to cyberhex0339@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: Cyber HeX Data Deletion Request.
  3. Explain that you want your information associated with the Cyber HeX app deleted.
  4. We may contact you to verify your request.

Disconnect Instagram

You can also remove the app's access to your Instagram account through your Meta account settings or the relevant Business Integrations settings.

Third-party services

If your information has also been processed or stored by Alpha Post or Meta, you may need to contact those services separately to request deletion from their systems.

Processing requests

We will review verified requests and handle information under our control in accordance with applicable requirements. Information held by independent third-party services is outside our direct control.
